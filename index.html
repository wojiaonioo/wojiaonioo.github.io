DEFgcm可微、等价、快速的海洋环流模式A differentiable, equivalent and fast ocean model

DEFgcm 用 PyTorch 实现 MITgcm 的海洋离散方案，由同一个程序计算模式积分及其梯度。在南海，它的积分与 MITgcm 之差，和只改变求和顺序的两次 MITgcm 运行之差相当。这些梯度用于四维变分同化，也能在线训练神经网络，包括时间步内部的网络。

DEFgcm implements the ocean discretizations of MITgcm in PyTorch so that one program computes integrations and their gradients. Over the South China Sea, its integrations differ from those of MITgcm about as much as MITgcm runs with reordered sums do. The gradients run 4D-Var data assimilation and train neural networks online, inside the time steps too.

结构Architecture

DEFgcm 在一个 PyTorch 程序中实现 MITgcm 的离散方案，既计算模式轨迹，也计算轨迹上代价函数对控制量的梯度。模式求解静力和非静力 Boussinesq 方程，支持经纬度和立方球网格，含 KPP 垂向混合、隐式自由面和开边界。

DEFgcm implements the MITgcm discretizations in one PyTorch program that computes a model trajectory and the gradient of a cost function on it with respect to the controls. It solves the hydrostatic and nonhydrostatic Boussinesq equations on latitude–longitude or cubed-sphere grids, with KPP vertical mixing, an implicit free surface and open boundaries.

一个时间步分五个阶段，反向计算对其中三处隐式求解解转置方程组。同化窗口按段求梯度：每段存一个起始状态，超出显存预算的放主机内存，反向时逐段重算、求导并释放。

A time step has five stages, and the reverse pass differentiates its three implicit solves through the transposed systems. The gradient of a window is computed segment by segment: the first state of each segment is stored, in host memory beyond a GPU budget, and the reverse pass recomputes, differentiates and releases one segment at a time.

PyTorch 编译器把张量运算融合成 GPU kernel，垂向求解、状态方程、混合和平流用手写 CUDA kernel。水平区域分解把每个水柱留在一张 GPU 上，反向时晕区交换把导数加回所属进程。

The PyTorch compiler fuses the tensor operations into GPU kernels, and hand-written CUDA kernels implement the vertical solves, the equation of state, mixing and advection. Horizontal domain decomposition keeps each water column on one GPU, and in the reverse pass the halo exchanges add the derivatives back to the owning process.

性能Performance

在南海的 N×N×90 测速网格上，从 N=480 起，4 张 A800 GPU 的静力单步耗时都短于 MITgcm，为其最短耗时的 0.50 至 0.81 倍。960×960×90 网格上，MITgcm 用 768 个 Xeon Gold 6132 核每步 126 毫秒，4 张 GPU 每步 77 毫秒，速度是前者的 1.6 倍；非静力配置下，4 张 GPU 每步 232 毫秒，MITgcm 用 400 核每步 1421 毫秒。

On the N×N×90 benchmark grids of the South China Sea, four A800 GPUs take less time per hydrostatic step than MITgcm on every grid from N=480 on, 0.50 to 0.81 times its fastest time. On the 960×960×90 grid, MITgcm takes 126 ms per step on 768 Xeon Gold 6132 cores and four GPUs take 77 ms, 1.6 times as fast; in the nonhydrostatic configuration four GPUs take 232 ms against 1421 ms for MITgcm on 400 cores.

N=960 上从单卡到双卡、从双卡到四卡都快 1.8 倍，N=1120 起从双卡到四卡快 1.8 至 1.9 倍；4 张 GPU 能容纳直到 2400×2400×90 的网格。一次代价函数加梯度的耗时是正向积分的 2.9 至 5.4 倍。

At N=960 the step runs 1.8 times as fast from one GPU to two and again from two to four, and 1.8 to 1.9 times from two to four on the grids from N=1120 on; four GPUs hold grids up to 2400×2400×90. A cost function and its gradient cost 2.9 to 5.4 times the forward integration.

四维变分同化4D-Var data assimilation

南海孪生试验：MITgcm 从 GLORYS 再分析起算得到真值，从 HYCOM 分析起算得到背景，DEFgcm 在 160×152×41 网格（含 KPP 和开边界）上同化从真值抽样加噪声的海面高度、海表温度和温盐剖面，同化窗 3 天，共 79 个窗口。

South China Sea twin experiments: MITgcm produces the truth from the GLORYS reanalysis and the background from the HYCOM analysis, and DEFgcm assimilates SSH, SST and temperature and salinity profiles drawn from the truth with noise, on a 160×152×41 grid with KPP and open boundaries, in 79 three-day windows.

窗口结束后第 3 天，预报误差 Q（温盐、流速和海面高度的归一化均方误差）的平均值由背景的 0.858 降到 4D-Var 的 0.505；按窗口计算，降幅平均为 40%，逐窗在 20% 至 57% 之间。初始时刻的分析增量与真实背景误差的相关系数中位数，海表温度为 0.85，温度为 0.67。

Three days after the window, the average forecast error Q (normalized mean-square error of temperature, salinity, velocity and SSH) falls from 0.858 for the background to 0.505 for 4D-Var; the reduction averages 40% and ranges from 20% to 57% in individual windows. The increments correlate with the true background error with median coefficients of 0.85 for SST and 0.67 for temperature.

混合建模：经模式在线训练网络Hybrid modeling: training networks online through the model

网络读入一个窗口的观测新息（观测相对背景轨迹的偏差）、背景状态和背景处的梯度，输出初始场。在线训练以该窗口的 4D-Var 代价函数为损失，梯度经 DEFgcm 的反向积分传到网络参数，不需要目标分析场；训练好后，每个窗口只需算一次代价函数及其梯度，不再做极小化。

A network maps the innovations of a window's observations against the background trajectory, the background state and the gradient at the background to the initial state. Online training uses the window's 4D-Var cost function as the loss, whose gradient reaches the network parameters through the reverse integration of DEFgcm, so no target analyses are needed; once trained, the network needs one evaluation of the cost function and its gradient per window instead of a minimization.

在训练期之后的 13 个测试窗口上，网络补上背景与 4D-Var 预报误差之差的 81%，逐窗为 72% 至 93%；同样代价下沿负梯度按拟合系数走一步，只补上 44%。

On the 13 test windows after the training period, the network closes 81% of the gap in forecast error between the background and 4D-Var, 72% to 93% in individual windows; a scaled step along the negative gradient at the same cost closes 44%.

在窗口结束后第 3 天的平均预报误差相同时，网络生成分析场的速度是 4D-Var 的 11.4 倍。

At the same mean forecast error three days after the window, the network produces an analysis 11.4 times as fast as 4D-Var.

同一反向积分也能对时间步内部的网络求导：一个有 1282 个权重、每步加上动量倾向的逐格点多层感知机，梯度与中心差分的相对差为 1×10⁻⁶ 至 2×10⁻⁶，加上网络后一次梯度计算只多用 8% 的时间。

The same reverse integration differentiates networks inside the time steps: for a pointwise perceptron of 1,282 weights that adds a momentum tendency at every step, the gradient agrees with central differences to a relative 1×10⁻⁶ to 2×10⁻⁶, and the network adds 8% to the time of a gradient evaluation.
